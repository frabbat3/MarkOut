Services & Features

Everything MarkOut does — free, private, and entirely inside your browser tab.

🟡

Highlight extraction

A custom YOLO26 model detects highlighted areas visually on every page — works with highlights made in Acrobat, Preview, Edge or any other reader, even if the PDF doesn't keep the annotations.

🔍

Scanned PDF support

The same neural pipeline handles image-only PDFs and screenshots: one flow for every document, digital or scanned.

📝

OCR in the browser

PaddleOCR recognizes the text of every fragment, self-hosted and offline-capable — no text ever leaves your machine.

📄

Clean Markdown export

Highlights come out in reading order, grouped by page and section, with YAML front-matter and stable citeable IDs.

🤖

LLM-ready output

The exported Markdown is designed as a base for AI assistants: stable structure, per-line IDs, extraction-quality notes.

🛡️

100% private

No upload, no account, no server. Processing happens locally; at the end the models and file are released from memory.

Who it's for

  • Students — turn highlighted papers into revision notes in seconds.
  • Researchers — build a clean corpus of passages from many PDFs, ready for analysis or LLM workflows.
  • Writers & editors — collect quotes and references without retyping.
  • Privacy-conscious users — any document, sensitive or not, stays on your device.

Platforms

MarkOut runs on any modern browser with WebAssembly: Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari 16+. Desktop gets optional WebGPU acceleration; mobile uses a memory-optimized pipeline. No installation, no account, no plugins.

Free forever. No sign-up, no paywall, no document limits. If you find MarkOut useful, consider starring it on GitHub.
